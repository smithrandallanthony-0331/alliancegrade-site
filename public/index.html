The Problem

Most partner programs were built reactively.

A portal here, an agreement template there, a certification course nobody finishes. Leadership knows something isn't working, but nobody can point to exactly what — or prove that fixing it will move revenue.

AllianceGrade replaces that guesswork with a number, a diagnosis, and a plan — built on 15 years of scaling ISV and channel programs at Epicor, Insight Software, and TCP Software.

The Methodology

Six pillars. Thirty criteria. One score.

Every pillar is scored against specific, evidence-based criteria — not gut feel — and weighted by how much it actually drives partner-sourced revenue.

PILLAR 01

Strategic Foundation & Executive Alignment

Is there a real strategy and an engaged executive sponsor behind the program?

PILLAR 02

Partner Recruitment & Segmentation

Are the right partners being targeted, tiered, and brought in — deliberately?

PILLAR 03

Enablement & Activation

Can partners actually sell and deliver independently once recruited?

PILLAR 04

Co-Sell & Pipeline Engine

Is pipeline being jointly generated, registered, and closed — repeatably?

PILLAR 05

Program Infrastructure & Economics

Are the contracts, economics, and systems built to scale, not one-off?

PILLAR 06

Partner Experience & Retention

Do partners stay, grow, and actively want to keep working the relationship?
